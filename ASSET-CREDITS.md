# Asset credits

## Photography

Illustrative furniture and interior photographs were downloaded from Unsplash's public image CDN and bundled in `public/images`. Exact source URLs are recorded in `asset-sources.json`.

Source and licence: https://unsplash.com/license

The Unsplash licence permits downloading and using images in commercial and non-commercial projects. The photos are included as visual assets within this storefront demonstration. Do not sell unmodified images or redistribute them as a standalone stock-photo collection. Replace the demo imagery with your own accurate product photography for a real catalogue.

## Fonts

- Manrope, bundled through `@fontsource/manrope`.
- DM Serif Display, bundled through `@fontsource/dm-serif-display`.

Their SIL Open Font License notices are included under `licenses/`. The website serves the fonts locally.
